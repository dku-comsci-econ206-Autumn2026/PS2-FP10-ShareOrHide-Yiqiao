## Defensible gap and contribution

The verified gap is narrow: the three closest papers do not, in the compared formulations, provide FP10's exact chain of (i) low/high confidentiality-cost types, (ii) Minimum/Partial/Full disclosure, (iii) publicly observed congestion, (iv) complete type-contingent pure Bayesian strategies, (v) a full-information welfare benchmark, and (vi) a congestion-contingent access-rule counterfactual with explicitly reported failure regions.

FP10's contribution is therefore a **reproducible, stylized comparative computational study** that connects private disclosure incentives, Bayesian equilibrium, welfare gaps, and a candidate access rule. It is a new application/model combination and a set of numerical comparative results within this project. It is **not** a new general theorem, a proof of optimality, or empirical evidence about drone operations.

The 2020 Los et al. paper is the most plausible match for the “Los et al. (2020)” novelty challenge supplied in the master brief.
