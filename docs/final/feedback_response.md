# Feedback response register

The machine-readable register is [`feedback_response.csv`](feedback_response.csv). Rows F01–F05 are faithful summaries from the author-supplied master brief, not quotations or independently recovered historical records. F06 is grounded in the supplied official course archive. F07 explicitly prevents false attribution of direct FP10 review.

Decisions use only `KEEP`, `REVISE`, or `INVESTIGATE`. A code- or result-affecting revision is tied to a rerun or a named open verification step. Historical dates and exact wording remain **HUMAN CONFIRMATION REQUIRED** where primary records are absent.
